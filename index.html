A South Indian kitchen, completely vegetarian

Guru Dosai is a pure vegetarian restaurant on Buthgamuwa Road in Kotte, built around one thing we love: a perfectly crisp dosai, served hot with sambar and fresh chutneys.

Our recipes follow the traditions of South Indian home cooking. We use fresh ingredients, grind our batter every day, and serve everything the way it is meant to be eaten: on time, hot and generous.

  • Every dish on our menu is vegetarian
  • Ghee roast, masala dosai and over 25 more favourites
  • Filter coffee brewed the traditional way

Our most-loved dishes

The plates guests come back for, week after week.

Masala Dosai

A golden, crisp dosai folded around spiced potato and onion. Our best seller.

Ghee Roast

Paper-thin, roasted in pure ghee until deeply crisp and fragrant.

Idli & Sambar

Pillow-soft steamed rice cakes with piping hot sambar and coconut chutney.

Medu Vada

Crisp outside, fluffy inside. Best dipped in sambar and chutney.

Ven Pongal

Creamy rice and moong dal tempered with pepper, cumin and ghee.

Filter Coffee

Strong, frothy and sweet, served the traditional way in a steel tumbler.
